How I work.

I like being the person who makes a team feel safe to ship. For more than ten years I've built test automation where there wasn't any yet, on the web, on phones and in APIs, from the first test to the CI pipeline that runs it every day.

What I care about most is the team that comes after me: suites they actually trust, code they can read, and a framework they can grow without needing to call me. At a recruitment SaaS startup I grew from engineer to Automation Lead, and when the company was acquired I was asked to stay and carry the suite across.

Today I lead Android automation in fintech, and I'm quietly teaching my everyday tools some new tricks with Claude and Playwright MCP. I work remotely from Cavite, in the Philippines.
